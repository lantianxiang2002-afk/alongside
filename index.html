技术支持 Support

同行是一个中英对照的圣经阅读 App，适用于 iPhone 和 Mac。遇到问题或有建议，欢迎联系我：

Alongside is a Chinese–English Bible reader for iPhone and Mac. For help or feedback, contact me:

Email：lanzong2002@126.com
Instagram：@leonard_ltx

通常会在几天内回复。请写明设备型号、系统版本和遇到的问题，有截图更好。
I usually reply within a few days. Please include your device, OS version and a screenshot if you can.

怎么使用 AI 大白话和讨论？

在“我的”里连接 AI：在 Mac 上可以直接登录 ChatGPT 账号；在 iPhone 和 Mac 上都可以填入你自己的 OpenAI 或 Anthropic（Claude）API Key，费用记在你自己的账户。不连接 AI 也能完整阅读经文、写笔记、划线和使用“此时此刻”。

Connect AI under Me. On Mac you can sign in with your ChatGPT account; on iPhone and Mac you can add your own OpenAI or Anthropic (Claude) API key, billed to your own account. Reading, notes, highlights and "For this moment" all work without AI.

当堂笔记怎么用？

在“笔记 → 当堂笔记”里新建。输入经文出处（比如“约 3:16”“约 3 16”，或只听清一半的书卷名），同行会提示经文，点一下就能插入。笔记可以导出为中文、英文或中英对照。

Create one under Notes. Type a reference such as "John 3:16" or "Jn 3 16" — even a half-heard book name — and Alongside suggests the passage to insert. Notes can be exported in Chinese, English or both.

怎么在手机和电脑之间同步？

两台设备都登录同一个同行账号即可。建议都用同一种方式登录：比如都用“通过 Apple 登录”，并且使用同一个 Apple ID。

Sign in to the same Alongside account on both devices, using the same sign-in method (for example, Sign in with Apple with the same Apple ID).

为什么退出账号后，书签和笔记不见了？

退出后，App 会切换到未登录时的本机资料；你的账号资料仍然保存在云端，重新登录就会回来。

After signing out, the app shows the data kept on this device without an account. Your account data stays in the cloud and returns when you sign in again.

换设备了，怎么带走资料？

登录同行账号会自动同步。也可以在“我的”里导出阅读备份，再在新设备上导入。

Sign in to sync automatically, or export a reading backup under Me and import it on the new device.

怎么删除账号？

在“我的”里点开账号，页面最下方有“删除账号”。删除后，云端的所有资料会永久删除。

Open your account under Me; "Delete account" is at the bottom. All your cloud data is permanently deleted.

隐私政策 Privacy Policy

生效日期 Effective date: 2026 年 11 月 10 日 · 10 November 2026

同行（Alongside Bible）由 Tianxiang Lan（Leonard）独立开发。我尊重你的隐私，只收集让 App 正常工作所需的最少信息。

Alongside Bible is made independently by Tianxiang Lan (Leonard). It collects only what it needs to work.

不登录时 · Without an account

你的阅读进度、笔记、划线、书签、收藏、计划和讨论只保存在你的设备上，不会上传到我的服务器。

Your progress, notes, highlights, bookmarks, favourites, plans and discussions stay on your device and are not sent to me.

登录同行账号时 · With an Alongside account

如果你选择登录，我会保存：

  • 你的邮箱地址（使用 Apple 隐藏邮件地址时为 Apple 提供的转发地址）和账号 ID，用于登录；
  • 你在账号页自愿填写的资料，比如称呼和你想记住的日子；
  • 你的阅读资料：进度、已读章节、当堂笔记、划线、书签、收藏的经文、读经计划、AI 大白话和讨论记录，用于在你的设备之间同步。

If you sign in, I store your email address (or Apple's relay address) and account ID to sign you in; profile details you choose to add, such as a display name and dates you want to remember; and your reading data — progress, notes, highlights, bookmarks, favourite verses, plans, plain versions and discussions — to sync it between your devices.

这些资料通过加密连接存放在 Supabase 提供的云数据库中，只有你自己的账号可以读取。API Key、ChatGPT 登录信息和提醒设置不会上传。

This data is stored over an encrypted connection with Supabase, our database provider, and only your account can read it. API keys, ChatGPT sign-in details and reminder settings are never uploaded.

AI 服务 · AI services

只有在你连接了 AI（登录 ChatGPT 或填入自己的 API Key）、并在 App 里同意之后，使用 AI 时，当前章节的经文、你的问题、本章之前的讨论和你设置的称呼，才会直接从你的设备发送给你选择的服务（OpenAI 或 Anthropic），不经过我的服务器。私人笔记不会被自动发送。这些服务按它们各自的隐私政策处理内容。你可以在 App 里随时撤回同意或断开连接。API Key 和 ChatGPT 登录信息只保存在设备的钥匙串或系统加密存储中。

Only after you connect AI (by signing in with ChatGPT or adding your own API key) and agree in the app, using AI sends the current passage, your question, earlier discussion for that chapter and your display name directly from your device to the service you chose (OpenAI or Anthropic), not through my servers. Private notes are never sent automatically. Those services handle content under their own privacy policies. You can withdraw consent or disconnect at any time. API keys and ChatGPT sign-in details are kept only in your device's Keychain or system-encrypted storage.

我不做的事 · What I don't do

  • 没有广告，不做跟踪，不使用第三方统计或分析工具。
  • 不出售、不分享你的个人信息。

No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics. Your information is never sold or shared.

你的权利 · Your choices

你可以随时在 App 里导出资料、退出登录或删除账号；删除账号会永久删除云端资料。也可以写信要求查看或删除你的资料。

You can export your data, sign out or delete your account in the app at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes your cloud data. You can also email me to access or delete your data.

儿童 · Children

同行不是专门面向 13 岁以下儿童设计的，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

Alongside is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
